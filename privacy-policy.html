1. Overview & Privacy Philosophy

Welcome to PCLink, developed by Beyond Edge ("we", "our", or "us"). PCLink is a dual-purpose mobile companion designed for:

  1. PC Master Cockpit: Seamless, ultra-low latency desktop control, trackpad, keyboard, 4K screen streaming, hardware telemetry, process monitoring, and file transfer with your own personal computer over your local private Wi-Fi network.
  2. Universal Appliance Remote: Direct hardware Infrared (IR) and local Wi-Fi control for Smart TVs, DTH Set-Top Boxes, Air Conditioners, Streaming Sticks (Fire TV / Roku), and Fans — with zero PC dependency.

Our Core Privacy Guarantee: PCLink operates on a strict 100% Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Local Area Network (LAN) architecture. We do not operate central cloud databases, remote relay servers, tracking telemetry, or user analytics. Your files, keystrokes, notifications, screen pixels, and remote commands NEVER leave your local network and are never transmitted to us or any third party.

4. Universal Remote (IR Blaster & Local Wi-Fi)

The Universal Remote module allows direct hardware control of household consumer appliances. Here is how privacy is preserved during remote operation:

  • Complete PC Decoupling: The Universal Remote is 100% decoupled from the PC Master server. When controlling a TV, Sun Direct DTH box, AC, or fan, the app sends commands strictly to the appliance. Your PC volume and controls remain completely unaffected.
  • Direct Hardware Infrared: For IR-equipped devices, signals are modulated locally by the phone's physical IR diode. No internet access or network traffic is generated.
  • Local Subnet Smart Control: For Smart TVs (LG webOS, Samsung Tizen, Sony Bravia, Mi TV) and streaming sticks (Amazon Fire TV ADB, Roku ECP), commands are sent directly to the appliance's local IP address on your private home router. Nothing is routed outside your local subnet.
🖥️

5. Screen Mirroring & Media Projection

When utilizing the Screen Mirroring feature (Phone-to-PC or PC-to-Phone):

  • Explicit User Consent: Screen capture can only be started when you explicitly grant system permission via Android’s standard MediaProjection consent prompt.
  • In-Memory Processing: Video frames are encoded in volatile memory (RAM) and streamed point-to-point over your local Wi-Fi connection directly to the paired PC Master desktop window.
  • Zero Local Recording or Archiving: The app does not save video files, screenshots, or logs to disk during mirroring unless you deliberately press the manual file transfer / screenshot button yourself.
🔔

6. Notification Sync Service

PCLink includes an optional NotificationListenerService designed to mirror selected mobile alerts (e.g., incoming call caller ID, SMS text preview, app notifications) directly to your PC desktop tray so you don't miss important messages while gaming or working with headphones on.

  • Completely Optional: This feature is disabled by default and requires explicit manual activation in Android system settings.
  • Local Transmission Only: Notification content is converted into an encrypted local WebSocket payload and sent directly to your PC server IP.
  • No Cloud Storage: Notifications are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party database. Once rendered on your PC screen, they are discarded from memory.
🔒

7. Data Security & Local Network Safety

We implement multiple layers of security to safeguard your local device interactions:

  • Optional Security Token: You can configure a secret authentication passphrase / security token on both the PC Master server and the PCLink Android client. All unauthorized socket connections lacking this token are immediately rejected.
  • Private Network Boundary: By default, the PC Master server listens only on local private network adapters (e.g., 192.168.x.x or 10.x.x.x) and does not open UPnP or public WAN ports.
  • Local Preference Storage: All user settings (such as paired IP addresses, port numbers, favorite remote layouts, and theme preferences) are stored strictly on your local device using Android's private EncryptedSharedPreferences.
